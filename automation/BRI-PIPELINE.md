# Bri (@briannaxx_01) content pipeline

Status: **paused in automation** (owner posts manually). If automation is switched on later, every post must carry the platform AI label.

## Goal
Grow Bri's following and send it to the owner's Fanvue, using clothed, non-explicit content. Revenue comes from Fanvue subscribers, so every post has a job: reach, follow, or click.

## Setup checklist
- [ ] Bio says she is an AI character, plus one line on what she posts, plus the Fanvue link (use a tracking link from Fanvue: `create-tracking-link`, one per platform).
- [ ] Highlights/pinned post: "Who is Bri" (AI character, what to expect, Fanvue link).
- [ ] Connect `briannaxx_01` as a Metricool brand for analytics (and posting later).
- [ ] Keep a separate Fanvue tracking link per post type so we can see which converts.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| Pillar | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle / outfit | 40% | the beach/lounge looks, "Sunday mood" captions |
| Personality / story | 25% | motivational story, day-in-the-life of the character |
| Polls & questions | 20% | "blond or highlights?", "what should I wear Friday?" (drives comments) |
| Fanvue teaser | 15% | clothed preview + "more on my page", tracked link |

## Weekly cadence
- Instagram: 4 feed posts + 3-5 stories. TikTok: 3-4 short videos. X: 1/day, text + image.
- Best times (Pacific): ~10am and ~6pm, Tue-Fri strongest.
- Reels/video get the most reach (the account's spikes were reels). Aim for 2 reels a week.

## Caption templates (flirty, clothed, honest)
- Lifestyle: "Golden hour and zero plans. Where should I go next? 🌴"
- Poll: "Team blond waves or beach curls? Vote below 👇"
- Story: "Started this page with nothing but an idea. Day 1 of showing up every single day."
- Teaser: "Fresh set just dropped on my page. Link in bio 💙"
- Reply to comments: short, warm, emoji, ask a question back.
Rules: never say she's a real woman, never invent a real-life backstory as fact (gym, job, city), no nudity/explicit text, no "DM me" bait.

## Fanvue and DM handling
- Fanvue requires the account be honest about AI; chats and messages must not claim she's human.
- Use Fanvue tools only for analytics and posts the owner approves (earnings, subscribers, top spenders). No mass messages without owner sign-off.

## Growth loop (weekly)
1. Pull Metricool analytics for the week; note top 3 posts by reach and by link clicks.
2. Repeat the winning pillar/format next week; drop the bottom pillar.
3. Review Fanvue tracking-link clicks to subscribers; adjust the teaser style.
4. Update this file with one-paragraph learnings.
