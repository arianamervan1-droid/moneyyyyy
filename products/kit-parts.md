# Part 7 — The 4-Week Starter Plan

You've done your first week. Now you build on it. This plan uses the same three workouts (A, B and C) for four weeks. You're not chasing a new exercise every day. You're getting better at a few simple ones.

**Schedule:** three non-consecutive days a week (for example Mon / Wed / Fri). If you miss a day, do that workout next and carry on. Don't double up.

## How to pick your weight

- Choose a weight where you could do about **2 more reps** after your last rep. That's effort 8 out of 10.
- If all your sets hit the top of the rep range with smooth form for **two workouts in a row**, add the smallest weight increase available next time.
- If form breaks down, drop the weight. Good form beats a heavier weight.
- Pain is not progress. Sharp pain means stop that exercise and use the swap. If it keeps happening, check with a qualified medical professional.

## The three workouts

**Workout A**

| Exercise | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 2 × 8–10 | 3 × 8–10 | 3 × 8–10 | 3 × 10–12 |
| Machine chest press | 2 × 8–10 | 3 × 8–10 | 3 × 8–10 | 3 × 10–12 |
| Seated cable row | 2 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 12 |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 2 × 10 | 2 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 10 |
| Plank | 2 × 20 s | 2 × 25 s | 2 × 30 s | 3 × 30 s |

**Workout B**

| Exercise | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leg press | 2 × 10–12 | 3 × 10–12 | 3 × 10–12 | 3 × 12–15 |
| Lat pulldown | 2 × 8–12 | 3 × 8–12 | 3 × 8–12 | 3 × 10–12 |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | 2 × 10 | 2 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 10 |
| Glute bridge or hip thrust | 2 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 10 | 3 × 12 |
| Dead bug | 2 × 6 per side | 2 × 8 per side | 2 × 8 per side | 3 × 8 per side |

**Workout C**

| Exercise | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Step-up or split squat | 2 × 8 per side | 3 × 8 per side | 3 × 8 per side | 3 × 10 per side |
| Incline push-up or machine press | 2 × 8–12 | 3 × 8–12 | 3 × 8–12 | 3 × 10–12 |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 2 × 10 per side | 3 × 10 per side | 3 × 10 per side | 3 × 12 per side |
| Cable face pull | 2 × 12–15 | 2 × 12–15 | 3 × 12–15 | 3 × 12–15 |
| Farmer carry | 2 × 30 s | 3 × 30 s | 3 × 30 s | 3 × 40 s |

Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Warm up for 5 minutes before every workout.

## Cues for the key moves

- **Goblet squat:** hold one dumbbell at your chest, elbows inside your knees, chest tall, heels down.
- **Romanian deadlift:** push your hips back like closing a car door with your hips, soft knees, flat back, dumbbells close to your legs.
- **Row (cable or dumbbell):** pull your elbow toward your ribs; don't shrug.
- **Lat pulldown:** lean back slightly and pull to your upper chest.
- **Glute bridge:** feet flat, ribs down, squeeze your glutes and pause for one second at the top.
- **Farmer carry:** stand tall, shoulders down, walk with short, steady steps.

## Swaps: when something's busy, hurts, or is too hard

| If you can't or don't want to do… | Try… |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat | Box squat to a bench |
| Leg press | Sit-to-stand from a bench |
| Chest press | Incline push-up on a bench |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | Seated machine shoulder press |
| Lat pulldown | Assisted pull-up machine (lighter) |
| Seated cable row | Chest-supported row |
| Romanian deadlift | Hip hinge with a broomstick, then light dumbbells |
| Step-up | Lower step, or hold a rail |
| Plank | Incline plank on a bench |
| Dead bug | Bent-knee heel taps |

## After week 4

If it felt good, repeat the four weeks and aim to add a little weight or a rep or two. If it felt easy, keep the same plan and add one set to the first exercise of each workout. Staying with the same plan for a few cycles is fine. Boring and consistent beats exciting and quit.

# Part 9 — Simple grocery list and meal ideas

This is general food information to make planning easier. It isn't a diet, a calorie target or personal nutrition advice. If you have a medical condition, allergies, or are pregnant, check with your doctor or a registered dietitian.

## A simple shopping list (one week, flexible)

**Proteins (pick 2–3):** eggs, chicken breast or thighs, ground turkey, canned tuna, salmon, tofu, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, beans or lentils.

**Carbs (pick 2–3):** rice, oats, potatoes or sweet potatoes, whole-grain bread or wraps, pasta, fruit.

**Veggies (pick 3–4):** spinach or mixed greens, broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, frozen vegetable mix, tomatoes.

**Fats and flavor:** olive oil, nuts or nut butter, avocado, salsa, soy sauce, spices you like.

**Convenience:** frozen vegetables, microwave rice, pre-cooked chicken, canned beans. These count.

## Easy meal ideas

- **Breakfast:** eggs and toast; oats with fruit and yogurt; Greek yogurt with granola.
- **Lunch:** chicken, rice and vegetables; tuna wrap; burrito bowl with beans.
- **Dinner:** sheet-pan chicken and potatoes; stir-fry with frozen vegetables; pasta with ground turkey and tomato sauce.
- **Snacks:** fruit and nuts; cottage cheese; hummus and carrots; a protein shake if you like.

## Make it easier

1. Pick **one** shopping day.
2. Cook one protein and one carb in bulk, mix them with different veggies and sauces.
3. Keep two "no-cook" meals ready for busy days.
4. Don't aim for perfect. Aim for a plan you'll actually follow.
